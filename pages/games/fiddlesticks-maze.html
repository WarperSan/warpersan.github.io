FiddleSticks' Maze

This project was made as a project for school. The player needs to find three tombstones that, once interacted with, will defeat the boss.

The boss lurks the player, trying to defeat the player without being seen. All enemies are powered by behaviour trees. The maze is randomly generated.

Everything was made by me and my colleague. Although it was a teamwork, I mostly handled the technical structure of the project while he handled the creative aspect of the project.

Controls

  • WASD to move
  • Mouse to look around
  • Left click to interact
This game is compatible on PC.
