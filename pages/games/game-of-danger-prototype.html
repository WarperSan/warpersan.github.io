Game of Danger

This project was made as a challenge to myself. I had an idea of a gimmick and tried to make a small prototype about it. The player needs to complete the puzzles of the first world.

This game tries to make the player think outside the box. With both 3D and 2D, the puzzles can use that new dimension to explore and create interesting puzzles.

Controls

  • WASD to move
  • Mouse to look around
  • Left click to interact
  • More controls in the game itself
This game is compatible on PC.
